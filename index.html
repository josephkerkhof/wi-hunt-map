Install Field Hours

Field Hours then opens from your Home Screen and works with no signal.

  1. Tap More next to the address bar. Then tap Share

    If Share is already on the toolbar, tap it.

  2. Tap Share on the toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add. If you see Open as Web App, keep it on.
  5. Open Field Hours from your Home Screen.

Don’t see Add to Home Screen? Open this page in the Safari app first.

  1. Tap Share. In some browsers, Share is in the Menu
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add.
  4. Open Field Hours from your Home Screen.

This page is open inside another app. Open it in Safari to add it to your Home Screen.

  1. Tap the Menu at the top or bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap Open in Safari or Open in browser.
  3. Safari opens with the next steps.
  1. Tap the browser Menu
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Install or Add.
  4. Open Field Hours from your Home screen.
